Benefits of AI

Helping people learn

AI powered tools can explain topics in different ways until something clicks, offer practice questions, and give instant feedback- making it easier for students to learn at their own pace.

Saving time on repetitive tasks

AI can sort emails, transcribe aiudio, summarise long documents, and handle other repetitive work quickly, freeing people up for tasks that need human judgement and creativity.

Supporting healthcare

AI can help doctors detect disease earlier by spotting patterns in scans and test results that might be easy for a person to miss.

Making technology more accessible

Features like live captions, text-to-speech, and translation tools use AI to help people with disabilities or language barriers use technology more easily.
