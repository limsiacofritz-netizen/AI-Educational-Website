Risks of AI

Bias and unfairness

AI learns from data created by humans, so it can pick up and repeat unfair patterns already present in that data — for example, treating some groups of people differently without meaning to.

Privacy concerns

Many AI tools are trained on large amounts of personal data, which raises questions about who owns that data and how it's being used.

Misinformation

AI can generate text, images, and video that look real but aren't, making it easier to spread false information if people aren't careful about checking sources.

Job changes

As AI takes over some repetitive tasks, certain jobs may change or disappear, while new kinds of jobs are created — this shift can be difficult for workers to adapt to.
